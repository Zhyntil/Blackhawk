Returns

With the exception of the overstock, each order that is placed is made to order therefore, we cannot accept returns unless there are defects in the product.

We work diligently to ensure that each order is made to our exacting standards, but things can go wrong on rare occasion. If this is the case, please call us and email a photo of the issue and we will be happy to have you return the defective products for replacement.
