About Us

We wish to thank you for taking the time to review our company's products and information. Blackhawk Trading Co., Inc. is a minority-owned, equal opportunity employer, located in Colorado, producing quality American-made metal stamped and pewter products.

Founded in 1984, Blackhawk Trading Co. has spent four decades mastering the art of creating fine stamped metal pieces in sterling silver, nickel silver or brass. Our reputation has been built on quality craftsmanship, assuring our customers receive the finest quality product.

We manufacture an extensive line of buttons and conchos using our original, copyrighted designs, as well as custom, signature designs for branding your merchandise with your company logo, in a variety of sizes from 1/2" to 1 1/2".

We use the finest sterling silver, nickel silver, brass, copper and pewter for all of our products.

We can apply your logo, or create a custom design to manufacture buttons, conchos, earrings, hat bands, belts and so much more! We also create buttons from natural antler and genuine antique U.S. coins, as well as other available world currency coins.

No order is too small or too large, and we ship all over the world. Please email us with any questions, or for a quote on current metal pricing.
